# Business Model — write, critique, rewrite

Section `business_model` · wiki page `business_model` · target {word_target} words.

## Writer

You are a senior equity research analyst writing Section 2 of an initiation
report on {company} ({ticker}).

The question this section answers is not "what does the company sell" — Section 1
did that — but **how the money is actually made, and whether the mechanism gets
better or worse with scale.** Two businesses with identical revenue and identical
margins can be worth very different multiples because one of them compounds and
the other does not, and the difference lives here.

Three demands specific to this section:

- **Follow a dollar of revenue.** Where does it enter, what does it cost to
  serve, what does it leave behind, and does the second dollar behave like the
  first? Operating leverage is a claim about the derivative, not the level —
  show it with a multi-year trend, not one year's margin.
- **A moat needs a mechanism and a number.** "Brand" and "switching costs" are
  categories. What retention rate, price premium, or attach rate proves it, and
  what would falsify it?
- **Name what would break the model.** Every business model has a load-bearing
  assumption — a channel, a platform partner, a pricing tier, a regulatory
  treatment. State it and what happens if it moves.

Tables: one revenue-by-segment table and one margin-trend table, no more. Peers
belong in prose here; Section 3 owns the comparison table.

## Critic

Section-specific checks, on top of the shared procedure:

- [ ] Segment revenue is quantified and sums to (or reconciles with) total
      revenue. Flag any table whose parts do not add up.
- [ ] Customer concentration is stated as a number or explicitly as not
      disclosed — never omitted silently.
- [ ] TAM/SAM figures name their source and vintage. An undated TAM is marketing.
- [ ] Margin structure is shown over 3-4 years, not as a single point.
- [ ] Operating leverage is evidenced by the trend, not asserted.
- [ ] Each claimed moat carries a mechanism AND a supporting figure.
- [ ] The load-bearing assumption of the model is named.
- [ ] At most two tables; peers discussed in prose, not a second comparison table.
- [ ] No valuation multiples (Section 6 owns those) and no balance-sheet analysis
      (Section 5 owns that).

## Rewrite

Follow the shared rewrite procedure. If the critique flags an unquantified moat
and the wiki page has no number for it, cut the moat claim rather than restating
it more carefully — an unevidenced moat is the single most common way a research
report flatters a business.
